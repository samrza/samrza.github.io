challenge 1 : learning world flags

6 september 2026 · 5 min read
  • Challenge: #1 — Learn World Flags
  • Difficulty: 6/10
  • Equipment: Laptop/phone + internet
  • Method: Quizzes + Anki + trial and error
  • Location: Indoor
  • Cost: Free

for this challenge, i decided to learn the flags of the world.

i mainly used jetpunk, literally 100-200 times, to repeatedly test myself and learn through trial and error.

i also used anki web for spaced repetition, but the most important part was simply trying, getting flags wrong, and trying again.

how i learnt the flags

i used different silly memory techniques and mnemonics to remember difficult flags.

for example, i used the grenade-like structure on the grenada flag as a visual mnemonic.

i also used categorization to group similar flags, like monaco, indonesia and poland.

these small memory tricks made it easier to distinguish flags that initially looked almost identical.
